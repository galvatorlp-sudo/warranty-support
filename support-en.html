Warranty Support

For questions, problems, or feedback, contact us at development@gutensohn.eu.

Information that helps us investigate

Please include the app version, your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not email complete receipts, serial numbers, or other confidential documents unless they are expressly required to handle your request.

Response and privacy

Support requests are answered as soon as reasonably possible. Information in a support email is used only to handle the request and troubleshoot the issue. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Apple Intelligence

AI features require a supported device, a compatible iOS version, and Apple Intelligence to be enabled. AI output can be wrong and does not replace verification with the retailer or manufacturer or advice from a qualified legal professional.
