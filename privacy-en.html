2. Scope

This policy explains how the Warranty iOS app and related support handle personal information. It applies to the German and English versions offered. Apple operates the App Store, iCloud, and system services under its own terms and privacy information.

3. Information processed and its sources

3.1 Content in the app

At your request, Warranty processes receipt images, PDFs, product photos you take or select, recognized text, product and retailer names, prices, purchase dates, invoice and serial numbers, warranty information, notes, and warranty cases. The content comes from your entries and from camera, photo, or file imports you expressly select. It is stored in protected app storage on the device by default and is not sent to a server operated by the publisher.

3.2 Apple Intelligence

On supported devices, Warranty uses Apple Foundation Models to structure receipt information and provide unverified warranty assessments and warranty-assistant answers. Warranty does not connect a publisher-operated or freely configurable external AI service for these features. Use and availability of the Apple system model depend on your device, iOS configuration, and Apple's terms.

3.3 Optional iCloud

If you enable iCloud in Warranty, Apple syncs app data through the private iCloud area of the Apple Account in use. The publisher does not operate a separate sync server. You can disable the feature; data already synced is also governed by Apple and iCloud settings.

3.4 App Store purchase

The lifetime Pro purchase is handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Warranty receives transaction and entitlement information supplied by Apple, in particular whether the entitlement is valid. The publisher does not receive complete payment or credit-card details.

3.5 Support

If you voluntarily contact support, we process your email address, technical information, message content, and any attachment you choose to send. Do not send complete receipts, serial numbers, or confidential documents unless they are expressly required to handle the request.

4. Purposes and legal bases

To the extent processing is legally attributable to the publisher, it is carried out for these purposes:

  • Providing the app, local document management, and the Pro entitlement to perform a contract or take steps at your request before entering one (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR or corresponding laws).
  • Optional features such as iCloud, camera, photos, files, notifications, and Face ID at your express request and with the relevant system permission (Article 6(1)(a) or (b) GDPR where applicable).
  • Handling support requests to perform the contract and for the legitimate interest in secure and reliable support and troubleshooting (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
  • Meeting legal duties and establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims (Article 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR).

You may withdraw consent for the future. System permissions can be changed in iOS. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

5. Recipients, service providers, and sale of data

Warranty uses Apple for App Store distribution, StoreKit, optional iCloud, and Apple system features. Support emails are transmitted and stored through technical email providers. Providers may process information only for the relevant purpose and under applicable privacy requirements.

There are no advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics providers. Warranty does not sell or rent personal information and does not share it for behavioral or cross-context advertising.

6. Processing outside your country

The publisher does not send app content to a publisher-operated server. When you use Apple services or communicate by email across borders, information may be processed outside your country, the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Canada, Australia, or New Zealand. Apple describes locations and safeguards in its privacy information. Where the publisher initiates an international transfer and is legally responsible, required adequacy decisions, contractual clauses, or other recognized safeguards will be used.

7. Whether information is required

Providing product data and permitting camera, photos, files, notifications, Face ID, or iCloud is voluntary. Without an item or permission, only the feature that depends on it is unavailable or limited. Manual entry remains available where possible. A valid App Store transaction is required for the Pro entitlement. A support request cannot be answered without contact information.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Local app data remains until you delete it in Warranty or remove the app and its data.
  • Deleting a product also removes its receipt when no other product uses it. Profile → Data lets you delete all Warranty data.
  • If iCloud is enabled, deletion syncs through Apple; additional backups and time limits are governed by Apple's services and settings.
  • Support correspondence is ordinarily deleted no later than twelve months after the request is closed, unless legal retention duties or claims require longer storage.
  • Apple retains purchase and transaction data under its rules. Warranty keeps only information needed to check the entitlement.

9. Security

Warranty uses protected app storage and iOS security features. You may optionally protect app access with Face ID. Although reasonable measures are used, no digital system can guarantee absolute security. Applicable breach notification and communication duties will be followed.

10. Your rights and complaints

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to notice, access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and withdrawal of consent. Send requests to development@gutensohn.eu. Identity verification may be required. You will not be discriminated against solely for exercising a statutory privacy right.

The publisher cannot access content stored only on your device. You can correct, export, or delete that content directly in the app.

You may complain to the authority where you live or work or where the alleged issue occurred, including the Austrian Data Protection Authority. Depending on your location, relevant bodies may also include the UK ICO, Swiss FDPIC, Canadian OPC or provincial authority, Australian OAIC, New Zealand OPC, or the responsible US state authority.

11. AI and automated decisions

AI output is unverified assistance. It does not automatically decide statutory rights, warranty coverage, purchases, prices, or access to the app and does not produce legal or similarly significant effects. Verify important information against the purchase contract, applicable law, and current retailer or manufacturer terms.

12. Children and other people's data

Warranty is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect children's information for advertising or profiling. If you import documents or photos, make sure you are entitled to do so and cover unnecessary information about other people where possible.

13. Additional information for international users

United States: Where a US state privacy law applies, the access, correction, deletion, and portability rights described above are available. During the preceding twelve months, personal information was not sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising. Warranty does not use sensitive information to infer profiles for advertising.

Canada: The privacy contact above is responsible for questions and complaints. Information is collected, used, and disclosed only for the stated purposes, and you may request access and correction. You may still complain to the federal or applicable provincial privacy authority.

Australia and New Zealand: Access, correction, or complaint requests may first be sent to the privacy contact. We will investigate and respond within the time required by applicable law. You may then complain to the OAIC or OPC as applicable.

14. Changes

This policy is reviewed and updated when features, providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be identified in an appropriate way. The current version is available at this address.
